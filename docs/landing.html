Cxeify turns your Corsair Xeneon Edge display into a fully functional Spotify remote control. Play, pause, skip tracks, and control volume — all from your iCUE dashboard.

What you can do

Everything you need to control your music from your display

Playback Controls

Play, pause, skip to the next or previous track directly from your Xeneon Edge display.

Volume Control

Adjust the volume with an interactive slider. Mute and unmute with a single tap.

Shuffle & Repeat

Toggle shuffle and repeat modes to control how your music plays.

Real-Time Updates

See the currently playing track, artist, and progress bar — updated in real time.

Album Art Display

The current album cover is shown as a blurred, customizable backdrop behind the controls.

Fully Customizable

Change colors, transparency, and update interval to match your setup perfectly.

How it works

No companion server required — just iCUE and your browser

1

OAuth Setup

Open the setup page in your browser, create a Spotify app, and authorize Cxeify to control your playback. The entire process takes about 2 minutes.

2

Install the Widget

Copy the Cxeify folder to your iCUE widgets directory and select it on your Xeneon Edge device in iCUE.

3

Enter Credentials

Paste your Spotify Client ID and Refresh Token into the widget settings. The widget connects automatically — no further configuration needed.
